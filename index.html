Production Standards & Technical Specifications

Every asset published by Mocap is audited for clean topology, real-world scale, zero missing references, and optimal real-time performance.

Ultra-Low Draw Calls

All 115 assets share just 3 PBR materials (Main Palette, Transparent Glass, Emissive) and two 256×256 uncompressed stylized color atlases—making mobile, VR, and desktop performance effortless.

1-Click Unity C# Editor Tools

Includes a custom Shelf & Store Builder Editor window that auto-populates shelves with products (with jitter, density & seed controls) and snaps modular architecture cleanly onto a 2.0m grid.

Custom Collisions & Clean Scale

Every mesh ships at real-world 1:1 metric scale, clean pivot placement, secondary Lightmap UV2, and custom UCX_ colliders automatically converted into Unity Box/Mesh Colliders.

Quick-Start Documentation & Customer Support

Getting started with Mocap Unity packages takes less than a minute.

1

Import & Match Pipeline

Import the package into your Unity project. If you use Built-in RP or HDRP instead of URP, click Tools > Cozy Shop Kit > Setup Shared Materials to auto-convert all 3 materials instantly.

2

Build on the 2.0m Grid

Drag prefabs from Assets/CozyShopKit/Prefabs/ into your scene, or open Tools > Cozy Shop Kit > Shelf & Store Builder to snap walls and generate modular room shells automatically.

3

Auto-Stock Shelves

Select any SM_Shelf_* or cooler prefab in the Hierarchy, click Auto-Find All SM_Prod_* in the builder window, and click Populate Selected Shelves to stock rows in one click.
